# Testing Mapifind (for testers — no tech experience needed!)

Thanks for helping test Mapifind! Here's how to get the latest version on
your Android phone. This only works on **Android** phones for now (not
iPhone yet).

## Getting the app

1. Anthony will send you a link (it looks like
   `https://expo.dev/artifacts/eas/....apk`).
2. Open that link **on your Android phone**, in your normal browser
   (Chrome, etc).
3. Your phone will download a file. Tap it to open once it's done
   downloading.
4. The first time you do this, Android will warn you it's blocking
   "install from unknown sources" — tap **Settings**, then turn on
   **"Allow from this source"**, then go back and tap **Install**.
   (This is normal for apps installed outside the Google Play Store — it's
   just Android being cautious, the app is safe.)
5. Once installed, open **Mapifind** like any other app.

## Getting updates

Every time Anthony makes changes, he'll send you a new link the same way.
Just repeat the steps above — it'll update the app in place (you won't lose
anything, and you don't need to uninstall the old version first).

## Giving feedback

While you're using the app, please tell Anthony:
- Anything confusing or hard to find
- Anything that crashed, froze, or looked broken
- Anything you wish the app could do
- Whether the map, path recording, and zone features make sense to you

Just send your notes however's easiest for you (text, call, email — whatever
you two normally use). There's no formal process — plain, honest feedback is
exactly what's needed at this stage.
